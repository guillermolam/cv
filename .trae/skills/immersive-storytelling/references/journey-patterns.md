# Journey Patterns

## Two-Lane Experience (Mandatory)
The portfolio must support two concurrent journeys:
- Recruiter fast path: immediate clarity and conversion (CV/contact).
- Exploration path: interactive discovery that deepens understanding.

Rules:
- Exploration must never hide the fast path.
- Any mode switch (e.g., “Explore” vs “Summary”) must be reversible and obvious.

## Reveal Strategy Defaults
- Default: reveal to clarify structure (section boundaries, hierarchy, relationships).
- Avoid reveals that delay reading (“wait for animation to finish”).
- Prefer stateful reveals that communicate system state (selected station, active layer, applied filter).

## Spatial Storytelling Defaults
- Use proximity, grouping, and transitions to communicate relationships between capabilities.
- Avoid decorative motion that does not map to story or state.
